Madloop works best on a computer.

It decodes and encodes video right here in your browser, which phones only partly manage. Open this page on a laptop or desktop for the full tool.


Turn any video clip into a seamless loop.

Madloop is a break-out feature from Madblur, a macOS visual synthesizer for looping animation. Madloop is free, open source and runs locally.
